# Knowledge provenance

Use the existing authorized knowledge surface. For code-dependent claims, record the
repository and revision or an explicit observation date; distinguish historical decisions,
current observations, assumptions and unresolved contradictions. Link primary artifacts
rather than copying transcripts, diffs or specifications.

Current-state documents may be updated to remain useful, with meaningful changes linked
to history. Historical decisions retain their context and a superseding pointer. Do not
force every note into an append-only wiki or preserve secret/personal data as history.

On reuse, check important claims against live state. Saved notes confer neither future
authorization nor automatic recall. A handoff carries the next task, settled choices,
remaining uncertainty and evidence pointers; it does not load every referenced artifact.
