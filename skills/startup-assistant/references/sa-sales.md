Lead on buying process, proposals, pipeline, onboarding, renewals, and churn. Sales is evidence about a buyer's situation and commitment, not an assumed funnel multiplier.

## Diagnose revenue quality

Identify the target customer, user, decision-maker, budget owner, urgency, alternative, approval/procurement process, and success condition. In consumer or transactional businesses adapt this to the actual purchase journey; do not force enterprise sales roles onto every customer.

Inspect funnel stage definitions, period, segment, conversion, time to close, losses, discounting, and source quality. Separate pipeline, verbal interest, signed bookings, recognized revenue, and collected cash. Weight pipeline only with defensible stage probabilities and time-to-close assumptions; otherwise present scenarios. A healthy coverage ratio depends on actual win rate and sales timing, not a universal multiple.

For a proposal, make the outcome, scope, exclusions, implementation effort, price, buyer obligations, proof, timeline, and next action clear. Never commit the business to a discount, SLA, delivery date, or contract term without authorization. Distinguish a proposed offer from an accepted agreement.

## Diagnose retention and service quality

Look at cohorts and segments instead of just an aggregate. Separate voluntary cancellation, payment failure, poor fit, missing value, onboarding friction, reliability, and service failures. Root causes need evidence from usage, support, interviews, and outcomes rather than a single cancellation label.

For recurring revenue, use the same starting customer cohort and period:

- GRR = (starting recurring revenue minus churned and contracted revenue) / starting recurring revenue.
- NRR = (starting recurring revenue minus churned and contracted revenue plus expansion) / starting recurring revenue.
- New-customer revenue belongs in neither numerator. A zero starting base makes these ratios undefined. Keep currency, period, and revenue definition consistent.

Expansion can hide losses in NRR; show gross retention and customer/logo retention when needed. For services or commerce consider repeat purchase, contribution, delivery quality, and time to value instead of forcing subscription ratios.

Choose interventions that address the cause: improve onboarding, fix reliability, clarify fit, change packaging, provide assistance, or adjust payment recovery. Retention does not justify coercive cancellation or hiding terms. For acquisition promises, check delivery and support capacity with operations.

Deliver the diagnosed bottleneck, supporting evidence, improved offer/process or retention intervention, proposed owner, leading measure, outcome measure, and follow-up decision. Drafting outreach does not authorize sending it.
