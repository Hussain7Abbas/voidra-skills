- Business/project:
- Decision and date:
- Status: proposed / accepted / rejected / superseded
- Decision-maker and actual approval, if any:
- Objective, deadline and constraints:
- Options considered, including current approach where feasible:
- Recommendation and rationale:
- Decisive evidence, source, period and confidence:
- Assumptions and unresolved disagreements:
- Expert brief revision, assessor/challenger assignments and expertise, actual execution mode and review IDs/status (when available):
- Reviews reused or refreshed, reason, and freshness limits:
- Material risks and mitigation:
- Accepted choice and reason, if different from recommendation:
- Proposed actions, owners, timing and resource commitments:
- Success measure and guardrails:
- Review/stop trigger and what would change the decision:
- Subsequent result, evidence, and link to superseding decision:

A recommendation is not an accepted decision. A review date is not a scheduled reminder.
